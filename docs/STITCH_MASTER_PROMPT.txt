[POSITIVE SPECIFICATIONS — MANDATORY RULES]
1. SPACING & DENSITY (4px Scale):
   - All spacing must be multiples of 4px: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 48px.
   - Sidebar padding: 16px (p-4). Card padding: 20px-24px (p-5 or p-6). Page margin: 24px-32px.
   - Gestalt rule: Group internal gap (4px-8px) must be 1/3 of outer separation gap (24px).
   - Navigation row height: 32px-36px with 2px-4px row gap.

2. COLOR & SURFACES:
   - Built on ONE neutral ramp (Graphite, Warm Neutral, or Ink Slate).
   - 95%+ of the screen must remain neutral. Color belongs exclusively to data and moments.
   - Elevation is created via discrete background steps and 1px crisp hairlines (border border-white/8 or border-black/8).
   - Exactly THREE text roles:
     * Primary: High contrast (e.g. text-zinc-100 or text-zinc-900)
     * Secondary: Contextual supporting text (e.g. text-zinc-400 or text-zinc-600)
     * Muted: Captions, metadata, keycaps (e.g. text-zinc-500) with 4.5:1 min contrast.
   - Exactly ONE primary accent color per screen, used only on 1-2 focal interactions.
   - On dark mode, primary CTA button uses high-contrast neutral off-white fill with dark text (bg-zinc-100 text-zinc-950), NOT a loud neon saturated button.

3. ICONS:
   - Single line-icon family (Lucide or Material Symbols Outlined).
   - Stroke width locked to 1.5px or 2.0px. Optical box fixed (w-5 h-5 flex items-center justify-center).
   - Icons inherit text role color (currentColor); never use multi-colored icon fills.

4. MICROCOPY & METRICS:
   - Short, direct labels ("Search", not "Search anything...").
   - Comparative deltas must have a baseline: "+14.2% vs last month" (never "+14%!!").

5. TYPOGRAPHY & NUMERIC ALIGNMENT:
   - Sentence case everywhere for titles, tabs, buttons, and labels.
   - All numbers, currency, timestamps, and table columns MUST use tabular numbers (font-mono or font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums).
   - Left-align text labels; right-align numeric and currency data in one clean column.

6. DETAILS & RADII:
   - Concentric nested radii: R_inner = R_outer - Padding (minimum 4px).
   - Keyboard shortcuts use semantic <kbd> keycaps (border border-white/10 bg-white/5 font-mono px-1.5 py-0.5 text-xs rounded).
   - Data charts: Neutral muted grid with only the single primary series highlighted in the accent color.

7. MOTION:
